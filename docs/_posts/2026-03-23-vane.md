Tired of feeding your research queries to big tech? Vane gives you the sophisticated AI-powered search experience you want (think Perplexity or ChatGPT search) but runs entirely on your infrastructure. No data leaves your network, no search history gets sold, no privacy compromises.

What sets it apart is the flexibility: plug in local models via Ollama for complete independence, or connect to OpenAI/Claude when you need the horsepower. It pulls from multiple search engines via SearxNG, handles file uploads for document analysis, and delivers properly cited answers with sources you can verify. The UI includes smart widgets for weather, calculations, and quick lookups, plus different search modes optimized for speed vs. depth.

With 33K+ stars and Docker deployment ready, this is clearly resonating with developers who want AI search without the surveillance capitalism. The setup looks straightforward, and you get the full feature set of commercial AI search tools without recurring API costs or privacy trade-offs.
