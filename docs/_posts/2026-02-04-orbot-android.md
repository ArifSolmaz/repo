In a world where your ISP logs every website visit and governments monitor internet traffic, Orbot gives Android users bulletproof anonymity. This isn't some sketchy VPN service that'll sell your data - it's the official mobile implementation of Tor, the battle-tested network that bounces your traffic through multiple encrypted relays worldwide. Your connection becomes untraceable, your location invisible.

Built in Kotlin by the Guardian Project (the folks behind secure communication tools for activists and journalists), Orbot works as both a system-wide VPN and selective app proxy. You can route all traffic through Tor or cherry-pick specific apps that need anonymity. The codebase is clean, well-documented, and includes proper build instructions with submodule dependencies. It's translated into dozens of languages and has millions of downloads on Play Store.

Whether you're researching sensitive topics, working around censorship, or just want genuine privacy without trusting a commercial VPN, Orbot delivers the real deal. The setup takes minutes, but the peace of mind lasts forever. Clone it, build it, or just star it to support digital freedom.
