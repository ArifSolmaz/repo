Ever wondered what happens to your digital life when the internet dies? Project N.O.M.A.D. is the answer - a self-contained knowledge fortress that runs entirely offline. Built for everything from remote expeditions to natural disasters, it packs local AI chat, complete Wikipedia mirrors, medical references, Khan Academy courses, and survival guides into one Docker-orchestrated system.

What sets this apart is the thoughtful curation and seamless integration. Instead of cobbling together separate tools, N.O.M.A.D. orchestrates Ollama for AI, Kiwix for offline content, Kolibri for education, and Qdrant for semantic search through a unified web interface. The one-line installer handles everything, and at 12,921 stars, it's clearly struck a nerve with the preparedness and self-hosting communities.

Perfect for remote workers, educators in connectivity-poor areas, emergency responders, or anyone who values true digital independence. The entire stack runs on a Raspberry Pi or old laptop, turning any device into a comprehensive knowledge station that works when everything else doesn't.
