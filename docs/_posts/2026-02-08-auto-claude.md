Ever wished you could just describe what you want built and have an AI team actually deliver it? Auto-Claude makes this reality by orchestrating Claude AI across multiple sessions to handle the full software development lifecycle. Instead of manually prompting and copying code snippets, you describe your project and watch as autonomous agents plan features, write implementations, and validate the results.

What sets this apart from simple AI coding assistants is the multi-agent approach with persistent memory across sessions. The framework maintains context between interactions, tracks progress on a Kanban board, and can work on complex projects that require multiple rounds of planning and iteration. With 11.5k stars and active releases across all platforms, it's clearly solving a real pain point for developers tired of babysitting AI coding sessions.

Perfect for indie developers, prototypers, and teams wanting to accelerate their development cycle. The TypeScript codebase is mature (v2.7.5 stable), cross-platform, and comes with both GUI applications and what appears to be a solid community around it. Worth trying on your next side project to see how much of the grunt work you can actually automate.
