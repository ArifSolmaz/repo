Tired of juggling multiple proxy apps on Android? NekoBox eliminates that headache by supporting virtually every proxy protocol you'll encounter - from classic Shadowsocks and HTTP(S) to cutting-edge Hysteria 2 and TUIC. Built on the robust sing-box core, it's become the Swiss Army knife for Android users who need reliable proxy connectivity.

What sets NekoBox apart is its comprehensive protocol support (15+ protocols) combined with flexible subscription parsing for popular formats like ClashMeta and v2rayN. The plugin system extends functionality even further with support for NaïveProxy and Mieru. With 18.6k stars and active development, it's clearly struck a chord with users who value both breadth and reliability.

Whether you're a power user managing multiple proxy setups or just need something that works with your existing subscriptions, NekoBox delivers. The project maintains transparency about licensing issues (avoid the compromised Google Play version) and offers extensive documentation. For Android developers interested in proxy toolchains or anyone seeking a unified proxy solution, this repository is essential.
