Ever hit Claude's usage limits right in the middle of an important coding session? This native Swift app sits quietly in your macOS menu bar, tracking your Claude usage across all your accounts in real-time. No more surprise "you've exceeded your limit" messages when you're deep in flow state.

What sets this apart is the multi-profile support - manage unlimited Claude accounts with separate tracking, automatic CLI integration that syncs with your terminal sessions, and a clean SwiftUI interface that shows session windows, weekly limits, and Opus-specific usage. The terminal statusline integration is particularly clever, displaying color-coded usage right in your command prompt with automatic profile switching.

At 888 stars and actively maintained, it's clearly solving a real problem for the Claude power-user community. Takes 30 seconds to install, works offline with local storage only, and includes 8 language localizations. Perfect for developers, researchers, and anyone who relies heavily on Claude and wants to maximize their usage without surprises.
