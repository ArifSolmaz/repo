That MacBook notch just sits there doing nothing while you're jamming to music or juggling files. Boring Notch transforms that dead screen real estate into something actually useful - a vibrant music visualizer that doubles as a control center, complete with AirDrop-enabled file shelf and calendar integration. It's like having a mini concert at the top of your screen that actually helps you work.

Built in native Swift, this isn't just another music widget. You get full media controls, a complete macOS HUD replacement, and practical features like drag-and-drop file management right in the notch area. The 7,100+ stars speak to developers who've been waiting for someone to make the notch pull its weight. The team even handles the unsigned app friction with clear setup instructions.

If you've got a notched MacBook and spend your day switching between music apps and file management, this is the kind of delightfully practical tool that makes you wonder why Apple didn't think of it first. One DMG download and you'll never look at that notch the same way again.
