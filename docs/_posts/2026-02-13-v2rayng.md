When you need a reliable proxy client on Android that actually works, v2rayNG delivers what others promise. With over 50,000 stars, it's become the go-to solution for developers and users who need robust network routing with support for the latest protocols like VMess, VLESS, Trojan, and Shadowsocks.

Built in Kotlin with dual-core support (Xray and v2fly), this isn't just another VPN app. It includes intelligent geo-routing with enhanced IP/domain lists, custom dat file support, and even runs on Android emulators and WSA. The active development shows - monthly commits, comprehensive wiki documentation, and a thriving Telegram community of users sharing configurations and troubleshooting.

Whether you're testing apps across different network conditions, need reliable proxy routing, or want to contribute to a well-architected Android networking project, v2rayNG offers both the stability of a mature codebase and the flexibility of open-source customization. The Golang-based core compilation process is well-documented for developers wanting to build custom versions.
