Remember when asking "What were our sales last quarter?" meant filing a ticket with the data team and waiting three days? WrenAI eliminates that bottleneck entirely. Just ask your question in plain English, and it generates accurate SQL, creates visualizations, and delivers AI-powered insights instantly. No more context switching between tools or hunting down the one person who remembers table schemas.

What sets WrenAI apart is its semantic layer - it doesn't just guess at your database structure like other text-to-SQL tools. The MDL (Modeling Definition Language) encodes your schemas, metrics, and relationships, keeping outputs accurate and governance-compliant. It supports everything from PostgreSQL to BigQuery, integrates with major LLM providers (OpenAI, Anthropic, Bedrock), and offers both self-hosted and cloud deployment options.

With 14K+ stars and active development, this is perfect for data teams tired of being the SQL bottleneck, or developers building internal tools who want to skip the custom dashboard nightmare. The Docker setup gets you running in minutes, and the API lets you embed GenBI directly into your existing applications.
