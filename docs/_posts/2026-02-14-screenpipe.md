Ever tried to find that website you saw last week, or remember what someone said in a meeting three days ago? Your brain forgets, but screenpipe doesn't. This Rust-powered tool continuously records your screen and audio, storing everything locally so you can search through your digital life using natural language queries like "show me that design mockup I saw yesterday" or "when did John mention the budget numbers?"

What sets screenpipe apart is its uncompromising stance on privacy - everything stays on your machine, no cloud uploads, no data harvesting. The OCR and audio transcription happen locally, and you can even integrate it with Claude via their MCP extension for more sophisticated AI interactions. With both a simple CLI install (`bunx screenpipe@latest record`) and a desktop app, it's surprisingly accessible for such powerful functionality.

The 16K+ stars and active community suggest developers are hungry for this kind of personal AI assistant. Whether you're debugging issues from last month, tracking down research sources, or just want a perfect memory of your digital activities, screenpipe turns your forgetful brain into an AI-powered search engine of your own life.
