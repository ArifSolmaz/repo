If you've ever tried macOS's built-in dictation and wanted to throw your Mac out the window, VoiceInk might restore your faith in voice-to-text. This Swift-built app promises 99% transcription accuracy using local AI models, meaning your voice never leaves your device - a refreshing change in our cloud-first world. The developer spent 5 months crafting this before open-sourcing it, and with 3,753 stars, it's clearly hitting a nerve.

What sets VoiceInk apart is its intelligent context awareness - it actually understands what app you're using and adapts accordingly. The 'Power Mode' automatically applies different settings based on whether you're in Slack, writing code, or drafting emails. Add global shortcuts, a personal dictionary for technical terms, and multiple AI-powered writing modes, and you've got dictation software that thinks ahead of you rather than behind.

While the source is open for developers to build and contribute, the polished app is available with a free trial at tryvoiceink.com. Perfect for developers who spend their days switching between code comments, documentation, and communication - finally, dictation software that gets the context.
