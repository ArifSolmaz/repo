Staying current in tech means monitoring dozens of platforms daily. This AI skill does the legwork for you, scanning 8 major sources (Reddit, X, YouTube, Hacker News, TikTok, Instagram, Bluesky, Polymarket) for recent discussions, then delivering synthesized reports with actual citations. Ask about "cursor vs windsurf" and get parallel research with side-by-side comparisons, or track "Arizona Basketball" to see both social sentiment and real prediction market odds (12% championship probability from 50+ markets).

The latest v2.9.5 adds Bluesky integration, comparative mode for A vs B queries, and auto-saves every briefing to ~/Documents/Last30Days/ as markdown files. Smart subreddit discovery finds relevant communities automatically, top comments get weighted scoring, and one ScrapeCreators API key covers Reddit, TikTok, and Instagram. With 455+ tests and 8,134 stars, it's become the go-to tool for developers who need comprehensive, cited research without the manual platform-hopping grind.
