Ever wished you could deploy specialized AI agents that actually understand your entire codebase and can modify it intelligently? AIOS Core does exactly that - it's a framework for creating AI agents that can orchestrate full-stack development workflows, from frontend to backend, with deep IDE integration across Claude, Gemini, Cursor, and more.

What sets this apart is the agent lifecycle management and cross-IDE compatibility matrix. You get pre/post-tool automation, context-aware guardrails, and actual code modification capabilities - not just chat suggestions. The 10-minute quick-start gets you from zero to a working agent that can greet you and execute commands. With 1563 stars and active development, the community is building agents for everything from creative writing to business strategy.

If you're tired of copy-pasting AI suggestions manually, this is worth exploring. The TypeScript/Node.js foundation makes it approachable, and the hook system means your agents can actually integrate into your existing development workflow rather than sitting alongside it.
