Finally, an AI coding assistant that doesn't force you out of your terminal workflow. Qwen Code brings the power of the open-source Qwen3-Coder model directly to your command line, offering 1,000 free requests daily via OAuth plus support for all major AI APIs (OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini). Unlike browser-based tools that lose context when you switch tabs, this stays put in your terminal where the real work happens.

What sets it apart is the agentic workflow with built-in Skills and SubAgents that can actually navigate large codebases, understand project structure, and automate repetitive tasks. Think Claude Code's capabilities but living natively in your shell, with VS Code/Zed/JetBrains integration when you need it. The fact that both the framework AND the underlying Qwen3-Coder model are open-source means they evolve together—no vendor lock-in, no mystery algorithms.

Perfect for developers who live in the terminal but want AI assistance without context-switching hell. One curl command gets you started, and with 18.9K stars in what appears to be rapid growth, the community momentum is real. The multi-language docs and professional installer scripts suggest this isn't just another weekend project.
