If you've ever spent weekends organizing scanned receipts, contracts, and invoices in paperless-ngx, you know the pain of manual tagging and OCR failures on messy documents. This tool transforms that tedious process by plugging AI directly into your document workflow—no more squinting at blurry scans or typing "Invoice_2024_03_something.pdf" for the hundredth time.

What sets paperless-gpt apart isn't just another AI chat interface—it's the LLM-enhanced OCR that actually understands context. Whether you're using OpenAI's vision models, Google Document AI, Azure Document Intelligence, or running everything locally with Ollama, it extracts text that traditional OCR misses and automatically generates meaningful titles, tags, and creation dates. The reasoning model support (like qwen3:8b) strikes an excellent balance between privacy and accuracy for those who prefer local processing.

With 2000+ stars and active development, this is clearly solving a real problem for the paperless-ngx community. The Docker setup is straightforward, and seeing your document chaos automatically organized feels like magic—the kind that actually saves hours every week.
