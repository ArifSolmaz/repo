Every Android keyboard from major tech companies is essentially a keylogger that phones home with your most intimate thoughts. FlorisBoard breaks this surveillance model with a completely open-source keyboard that keeps your typing data on your device where it belongs. With nearly 8,000 GitHub stars, it's become the go-to privacy keyboard for Android developers and security-conscious users.

Built entirely in Kotlin, FlorisBoard delivers modern features without compromise: gesture typing, customizable themes, multi-language support, and extensive layout options. The codebase is clean, well-documented, and actively maintained with regular releases through both stable and preview channels. Unlike proprietary alternatives, you can actually audit what runs between your fingers and your most private communications.

Whether you're a privacy advocate, Android developer, or just tired of keyboards that treat your typing as a data product, FlorisBoard offers a refreshing alternative. Installation is straightforward via F-Droid, Google Play beta, or direct APK - and the active Matrix community provides solid support for contributors and users alike.
