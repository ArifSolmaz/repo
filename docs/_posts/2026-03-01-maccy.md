If you've ever copied something important only to accidentally overwrite it with another copy, you know the frustration. Your macOS clipboard is amnesia incarnate - it forgets everything the moment you copy something new. Maccy fixes this fundamental limitation by quietly tracking everything you copy and making it searchable with a simple keyboard shortcut.

What sets Maccy apart from bloated clipboard managers is its laser focus on speed and simplicity. Hit ⇧⌘C and instantly search through your clipboard history, paste with or without formatting, pin frequently-used snippets to the top, or delete sensitive items. It's built with native Swift for macOS, so it feels like an Apple app and uses minimal resources. The 18K+ stars speak to developers who've discovered how much time they waste re-copying things.

Whether you're a developer juggling code snippets, a writer managing research quotes, or anyone who copies more than one thing per day, Maccy becomes invisible until you need it. Free, open source, and available via Homebrew - it's the productivity upgrade your Mac didn't know it needed.
