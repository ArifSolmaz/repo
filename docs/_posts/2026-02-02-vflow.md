Tired of repetitive tapping through the same Android workflows every day? vFlow turns your phone into a visual automation powerhouse. Instead of writing scripts, you drag and drop modules like "click here," "find text," and "if/then logic" to build sophisticated workflows that handle everything from daily check-ins to complex app testing sequences.

What sets vFlow apart is its "magic variables" system - outputs from one module automatically become inputs for the next, creating dynamic data flows that adapt as they run. The modular architecture means adding new automation capabilities is straightforward, while the Material 3 UI makes complex workflow editing surprisingly intuitive. It handles permissions cleanly, supports import/export for sharing workflows, and uses a clever Master-Worker process architecture for reliable execution.

Perfect for power users who want to automate their Android routines without learning scripting languages, or developers looking to study a well-architected Kotlin automation framework. With 663 stars and growing, the project shows solid momentum in the mobile automation space.
