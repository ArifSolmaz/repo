Your Android home screen is probably a mess of folders and forgotten apps buried in drawers. Kvaesitso flips the script entirely—instead of hunting through pages of icons, you simply search for what you need. Type a few letters and instantly find apps, contacts, files, calendar events, or even web results, all from one unified interface.

Built from scratch in Kotlin with Jetpack Compose, this isn't just another launcher skin. It features smart suggestions, customizable search providers, and seamless integration with your device's data sources. The 4,400+ stars reflect a launcher that actually reimagines how you interact with your phone, rather than just rearranging the furniture. Available through F-Droid with active development and proper i18n support.

If you're tired of the traditional app-drawer paradigm or you're the type who uses Spotlight search on Mac religiously, this launcher will feel like coming home. The codebase is clean, modern, and welcoming to contributors—perfect for developers who want to customize their daily driver.
